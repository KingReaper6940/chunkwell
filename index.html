THE UNIT MATTERS

One emoji. Several counts.

🙂 is 4 UTF-8 bytes, 2 UTF-16 units, 1 code point, and 1 grapheme. Choose the unit your destination actually uses.

EXACTLY YOUR TEXT

Every space has a home.

No overlap, numbering, trimming, or normalization. Join the parts in order to recover the text supplied to the splitter.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

A text tool, not a tokenizer.

No platform presets or token estimates. Links, sentences, code, and markup can cross boundaries. Review your parts before using them.
